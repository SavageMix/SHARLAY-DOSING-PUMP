SHARLAY Design System v1.0

Brand: SHARLAY

Tagline: Smart Dosing. Stable Results.

Design Philosophy

Luxury marine technology. The interface should feel calm and expensive.
Only active states, live data, and important actions glow.

Brand Identity

-   Custom wide-spaced SHARLAY wordmark.
-   Signature wave-cut A with cyan highlight.
-   The wave becomes a recurring UI animation across the app and
    hardware.

UI Principles

1.  One primary action per screen.
2.  Large readable numbers.
3.  Generous whitespace.
4.  Live data feels alive.
5.  Purple and Aqua are premium accents.
6.  Glass panels create depth.
7.  Every screen should feel calm.

SHARLAY Signature Experience

The home screen is Command — an orbital Reef Stability Arc sits over a
cinematic ocean wave animation, with live Alkalinity, Calcium, Magnesium
and Temperature metrics arranged around it. This becomes SHARLAY’s
instantly recognisable signature experience.
